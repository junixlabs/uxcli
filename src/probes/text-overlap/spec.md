# page.text-overlap · text painted over text · provenance opinion

- **why:** Two runs of text painted over each other cannot both be read. No WCAG success criterion names this at rest (1.4.12 covers it only under user spacing, 1.4.10 under reflow), so the commitment here is the instrument's own, stated once: *visible text is not painted over other visible text*. Provenance `opinion`; the card says so. Origin: junixlabs/uxcli#1, a positioned step counter painted over the first letter of a command name that three probes let through.
- **applies-when:** every pair of visible text boxes on the page. A text box is one client rect of a text node (`Range.getClientRects`) whose element is not hidden (`display`, `visibility`, effective opacity > 0), is painted on screen (rect inside the document, width and height > 0), is not inside a third-party subtree (`THIRD`), is not `aria-hidden`, and has no running animation or transition on itself or an ancestor. Pairs are skipped when either element contains the other, or when both boxes have the same nearest block container (lines and inline siblings of one flow: tight leading makes their content boxes touch without any glyph being painted over another; an absolutely positioned element is its own container), and when their nearest `position: fixed` or `sticky` ancestors differ (one is in a bar and the other is not, or they are in two different bars): that is content scrolled under a bar, a scroll state rather than a layout. Two texts inside the same bar are measured. Finite animations and transitions are given up to 2.5 s to finish before text is read; elements still animating are skipped.
- **correct-when:** for every pair, the intersection of the two boxes is smaller than 2 px in either axis, or smaller than 10 % of the smaller box's area. Both bounds must be crossed for a pair to count; touching or kerning-close boxes do not.
- **verdict:** `not-applicable` when fewer than two applicable text boxes exist; `fail` listing each overlapping pair (both elements, both texts, the intersection rectangle), at most 30 pairs; `pass` with the number of boxes and pairs checked.
- **invalid-if:** text still moving when measured (entrance animations): elements with running animations are excluded, and the runner measures after its settle. Text made to overlap on purpose as a design (knockout titles, watermark type) is reported as `fail`; the project suppresses it with a commitment entry when it disagrees, it is not silently skipped.
- **known-infidelity:** boxes, not glyph ink: side bearings and line-height padding are inside the box, which is why the 2 px and 10 % bounds exist. Rotated or vertical writing modes are measured on their axis-aligned bounds and may over-report. Two inline siblings of one block that are made to collide by relative positioning are not seen (same-block exclusion). Text inside `canvas`, `svg` or images is not seen. At most 600 text nodes are considered per page, document order.

## Revisions

v1 2026-09-08: written from junixlabs/uxcli#1 before the probe code; the falsification pair is the case from the issue (a step counter with `position:absolute` picking up the description span's `padding-left`) against the same list with the counter in its own grid column. Both twins carry adjacent two-column text (touching, not overlapping), a `<b>` inside a paragraph (containment), and an `ins.adsbygoogle` block whose two children overlap on purpose: the third-party exclusion must keep the probe silent on it.

v1.1 2026-09-08, first outside page (the project's landing draft): the only pair reported was a section heading scrolled under the sticky nav, read at the scroll position the runner left. Added the fixed/sticky exclusion above (first as "one side pinned", then by nearest pinned ancestor, because the heading was itself a sticky section header sliding under the sticky nav). The same run read only 44 of the page's text boxes because entrance transitions were still running; the probe now waits for finite animations (up to 2.5 s) before reading.

v1.2 2026-09-08, same page after v1.1 read all 474 boxes: the two lines of the display headline (two inline-block spans in one `h1`, line-height 0.98) were reported as a pair, their content boxes overlapping by 24 px while no glyph touches. Added the same-block exclusion above. Also fixed: boxes above the current scroll position were dropped as off-screen (viewport coordinates used where document coordinates were meant), which is why v1.1 read 44 boxes.

## Method status

`method-unproven`: no recorded run on pages the probe had not seen. A would-be fail is reported as `finding`, exit 0, until 20 unseen pages show 0 false fails.
