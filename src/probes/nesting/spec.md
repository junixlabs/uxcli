# page.nesting · a box inside a box inside a box · provenance research

- **why:** Every border, shadow or outline says "this is a separate thing"; three of them around one run of text say it three times, and a reader has to work out which one means it. Refactoring UI, *Use fewer borders* (Wathan & Schoger, https://www.refactoringui.com/): "Borders are a great way to distinguish two elements from one another, but they aren't the only way, and using too many of them can make your design feel busy and cluttered." Tufte, *Envisioning Information*, "1 + 1 = 3": two lines make a third, unintended visual element between them. NN/g, *The Principle of Common Region* (Harley, 2020): enclosing everything "creates visual clutter without benefit". The rule is theirs and named; the number — at most two nested boxes around a run of text — is the instrument's reading of "fewer", stated once here and in `rule`, which is why this probe's verdict is capped at `finding`.
- **applies-when:** every visible text run on the page (a non-empty text node whose element is displayed, not hidden, opacity > 0, painted at a size, not `aria-hidden`, not in a third-party subtree (`THIRD`), not `script`/`style`), at most 600 in document order. For each run, its chain is the ancestors up to `body` that enclose it: a border with width > 0, a style that is not `none` and a colour that is not transparent on two sides or more (one side is a rule between groups — a line, the middle step of space, line, box — and is not counted); a `box-shadow` that is not `none`; an outline with width > 0 and a visible colour. Left out of the chain: controls (`button`, `a`, `input`, `select`, `textarea`, `summary`, `label`, `[role=button|link|tab|menuitem]`) and anything inside them — a control's own edge marks an object, not a grouping — and table parts (`table`, `thead`, `tbody`, `tfoot`, `tr`, `td`, `th`), whose borders are a grid and a different rule. A background fill is not an edge: separating with a background shift is what the sources recommend instead of a border.
- **correct-when:** no text run has a chain of three or more. Two nested boxes (a card with a bordered field in it) are ordinary; three is a box inside a box inside a box.
- **verdict:** `not-applicable` when the page has no visible text run; `fail` (reported as `finding` while the method is unproven) listing each run at three or more, deepest first, one per distinct chain, at most 20, with the chain as selectors; `pass` with the number of runs and the deepest chain seen.
- **invalid-if:** the page is still animating (entrance transitions can paint a shadow that goes away): the runner settles animations first. A design that means the third box — a bordered callout inside a bordered dialog inside a bordered panel — is reported; the project turns the lens off or records the exception in its review, it is not silently skipped.
- **known-infidelity:** an edge the same colour as its background is counted (only transparency is read, not contrast); an edge drawn by a pseudo-element, an `<hr>`, an SVG or an image is not counted; two borders that do not enclose (top and bottom rules around a row) count as a box; `outline` is read at rest, so a focus ring is not counted. The chain stops at `body`, so a boxed `body` or `html` is not counted.
